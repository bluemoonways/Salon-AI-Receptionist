## The problem

When you are busy with a client or the salon is closed, calls go unanswered. Most callers do not leave a voicemail. They simply call the next salon.

## The solution

Ava picks up every call, talks to the caller in a warm and natural voice, and puts the booking straight into your schedule.

## A sample call

> **Ava:** Thanks for calling Glow & Co Salon, this is Ava. How can I help?
> **Caller:** Hi, can I get a haircut Saturday afternoon?
> **Ava:** Of course. I have two thirty, three, or four. Which works best?
> **Caller:** Three, please.
> **Ava:** Booked. See you Saturday at three.

The caller receives a confirmation number, and the appointment appears in your schedule automatically.

## What Ava can do

- **Book appointments:** service, stylist, day and time, all in one call
- **Reschedule and cancel:** callers confirm their name first, so only the right person can change a booking
- **Answer common questions:** prices, how long a service takes, opening hours, address, parking and payment
- **Respect your calendar:** your stylists' working hours, days off, breaks, holidays and closed days
- **Take a message:** if she cannot help with something, she takes the caller's name and number for your team

## What Ava will never do

- Make up a price, a time or a policy. She only uses the information you give her.
- Double-book a stylist.
- Give medical or skin-condition advice. She suggests the caller speaks to a stylist.

## How it works

1. **A client calls.** Ava answers and asks what service and day they want.
2. **She checks your schedule.** She only offers times that are really free.
3. **The booking is saved.** You see it in your schedule, and the caller gets a confirmation number.

## Your schedule, in a simple spreadsheet

Bookings are saved in a Google Sheet that you can open and read at any time. You do not need to learn new software. You can also edit your services, prices, stylists, hours and holidays directly in the sheet.

## Frequently asked questions

**Will callers know it is an AI?**
Ava sounds natural, but she is an AI assistant and will not pretend otherwise if asked.

**Do I need to change my booking software?**
No. Bookings are kept in a simple Google Sheet. If you use another booking platform, tell us which one and we can discuss connecting it.

**What happens if Ava cannot help?**
She takes the caller's name and number so your team can call back.

**How long does setup take?**
Once we have your services, stylists and hours, setup is quick.

**What does it cost?**
Your first 10 calls are free. After that, pricing depends on your salon size and call volume.

A Missed Call Is A Missed Booking

When you are mid-colour or closed for the night, callers usually move on to the next salon. Ava makes sure they book with you instead.

  1. A Client Calls

    Ava answers in a warm, natural voice and asks what service and day they want.

  2. She Checks Your Schedule

    She only offers times that are really free, based on your stylists, hours and days off.

  3. The Booking Is Saved

    The appointment lands in your schedule and the caller gets a confirmation number.

Questions Salon Owners Ask

Will callers know it is an AI?

Ava sounds natural, but she is an AI assistant and will not pretend otherwise if asked. Most callers just want a quick booking and are happy to get one.

How long does setup take?

Once I have your services, prices, stylists and opening hours, setup is quick. You will see a live demo first, so you know what to expect before going live.

Do I need to change my booking software?

No. Ava keeps bookings in a simple schedule you can open and read. If you use another booking platform, tell me which one and we can talk about connecting it.

What happens after the 10 free calls?

If you like Ava, we agree on a simple monthly plan. If not, you pay nothing and we stop there.

What does it cost?

Your first 10 calls are free. After that, pricing depends on your salon size and call volume. Send the form above and I will share exact numbers.

What if Ava cannot help with something?

She takes the caller's name and number so your team can call back. She does not guess on prices, availability or policies.

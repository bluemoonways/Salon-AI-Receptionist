Frequently Asked Questions

Will callers know it is an AI?

Ava sounds completely natural, but if asked, she acknowledges she is an AI assistant. Most callers appreciate the fast response and booking confirmation.

How fast is setup?

Once you provide your menu, pricing, and hours, deployment is fast. You will review a live demo first to ensure everything meets your standards.

Does it integrate with my software?

Ava maintains a clean, accessible schedule ledger. If you use a specific third-party booking tool, let us know to explore custom integration options.

What happens after the free trial?

If you love how Ava performs, we transition to your chosen plan. If not, there is no cost or obligation to continue.

How are calls charged?

Pricing scales cleanly based on your volume and plan. Submit the demo form for exact breakdowns tailored to your salon size.

What if a request is too complex?

For any request outside her scope, Ava logs the caller's details and message so your team can follow up directly.
